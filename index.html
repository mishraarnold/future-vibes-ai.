01 — OUR VISION

Big ideas should be easy to explore.

FUTURE VIBES AI is an independent project about making creativity feel more accessible. The goal is to build simple, playful tools that help people experiment, learn, and bring their ideas to life — even on a phone.

We're starting small, learning in public, and keeping this first experiment free.
